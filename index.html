WHY BRAINLEAGUE IS DIFFERENT
⚡

Competition = Pressure Simulation

Exams are timed and high-stakes. Practising under zero pressure trains the wrong reflex. Every battle has a live opponent, a 25-second clock, and ELO consequences — the same cognitive load as the real exam.

🔬

Feedback = Learning, Not Just Scores

After every battle you see exactly which questions cost you marks and which topics are consistently weak — ranked by miss rate. The same diagnosis a private tutor would give, automated and instant.

🔁

Rematch = Mastery Loop

One tap: "Train Weak Areas". The system loads your next battle with questions from your exact gap topics. Keep battling until the weakness disappears from your report.

THE REAL QUESTION

"Why not just practice questions on a revision site?"

✗

Kahoot, revision sites, and past paper apps let you solve questions at your own pace, with no timer and no opponent. You know it's just practice — your brain never activates exam mode.

✓

BrainLeague forces you to answer in 25 seconds, against someone who's trying to beat you, with ELO consequences. That's the same cognitive load as a real exam. The transfer to exam performance is direct.

→

Exams test speed + accuracy under pressure. The only way to train those is to practise under pressure. That's BrainLeague.
